## 3. Technical Readiness & Support Policy

Please adhere strictly to the following technical rules during the hackathon:

* **No Datasets Provided:** We are not providing any datasets (resumes, email logs, or video clips) to participants. Your team is responsible for sourcing, generating, or mocking realistic test data to demonstrate your solution.
* **Pre-Install All Software Beforehand:** All participants are expected to install and configure their required IDEs, Python/Node environments, libraries, and API keys before kickoff on Saturday morning.
* **No Software Installation or Setup Help:** Mentors will **not** assist with installing software, configuring local environments, or resolving dependency/machine setup issues.
* **Strict Q&A Scope:** Only questions strictly related to the hackathon and the 4 official problem statements will be entertained. No other technical or general support will be provided.
* **Mentorship Answer Windows:** You are free to post your questions in the **Mentor Help Channel** on Microsoft Teams at any time, **but please expect answers exclusively during the scheduled mentorship windows listed in Section 1**.
* **Model & Stack Freedom:** You do **not** need to train an AI model from scratch. You may freely use pre-trained open-source LLMs (Llama, Mistral, Qwen), local runners (Ollama), free-tier AI APIs (Gemini, OpenAI, Groq, Hugging Face), and established computer vision or audio libraries (MediaPipe, OpenCV, Whisper, Librosa).

## 4. Code Submission & Presentation Guidelines

### A. GitHub Code Freeze (`Sunday, Nov 15 @ 5:00 PM IST`)
Before the **5:00 PM IST** deadline on Sunday, your team must push all deliverables to your assigned private GitHub repository:
1. **Complete Source Code:** Clean, well-structured frontend/backend/pipeline code.
2. **Updated `README.md`:** Include your team name, selected problem statement, system architecture overview, tech stack used, and clear step-by-step instructions to run the project locally.
3. **Dependencies File:** Include `requirements.txt`, `package.json`, or `Dockerfile`.
4. **Presentation Deck:** Upload your final presentation slides into the repository (e.g., `/presentation/` folder).

### B. Live Team Presentations (`Sunday, Nov 15 @ 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM IST`)
* Each squad will deliver a live prototype demo and architecture walkthrough starting at **6:00 PM IST** using the standardized presentation slide format shared in Microsoft Teams.
* Focus your presentation on: (1) Problem & Architecture, (2) Live Working Demo, and (3) How Your System Handles Edge Cases & Explainability.

## 5. Evaluation Criteria

Projects will be evaluated by the judging panel across four core dimensions:

1. **Intent Understanding & Functional Accuracy (40%):** How accurately does the prototype solve the core problem statement and handle realistic inputs?
2. **Explainability & Edge-Case Reasoning (25%):** Does the system provide transparent, evidence-based explanations (rather than black-box scores) and account for real-world noise/edge cases?
3. **Architecture & Code Cleanliness (20%):** Modularity, smart use of pre-trained models/pipelines, latency awareness, and repository documentation.
4. **UI/UX & Live Demo Execution (15%):** Usability of the recruiter/reviewer interface and clarity of the live walkthrough.
